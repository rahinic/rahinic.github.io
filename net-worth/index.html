Net worth engine

What happens to €30k and €1,400 a month, over decades, for one invented person in Berlin?

Questions it will answer

  1. Data: can a deliberately messy German-bank CSV export be turned into a clean net worth timeline?
  2. ETFs: after Abgeltungsteuer, Vorabpauschale and TER, does a 5-ETF portfolio beat a 2-ETF one, in nominal and real terms, across 10,000 simulated paths?
  3. Gold: does an Indian gold scheme priced in INR beat German gold priced in EUR, once USD gold, USD/INR, EUR/USD and inflation are all moving, and does the answer change with the currency you plan to spend in?
