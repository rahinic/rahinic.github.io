Altersvorsorgedepot

From January 2027 the persona also pays €150 a month into the new state-backed Altersvorsorgedepot, modelled with the full rules: the state subsidy (up to €540 a year), the cost cap and tax only at payout. The rules come from consumer-finance summaries of the 2026 reform law, not from the law text, and are assumptions that could change.
