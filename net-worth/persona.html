Net pay is computed from gross with a simplified German tax and social-contribution function (source), using 2025 rules for tax class IV with no church tax, statutory health insurance and no children. The net figures above ignore inflation.

The brief asked for about €3,000 net a month. The function gives about €2,690, so every monthly amount below was cut by the same factor (about 0.897) to fit.

Costs grow with inflation. Contributions stay flat in nominal euros, so in real terms they shrink, except in the third scenario below. At the start there is no surplus. Pay is flat between raises while costs keep rising, so the gap widens and is covered from the savings account until the next raise.
